## 讨论范例

**Unit 2 Design** · 用于比较 AR/VR 的适用场景。

> AR is useful when people need digital information while working in a real space. A technician could see repair instructions overlaid on a machine and keep looking at the actual parts. VR is more useful when a team needs to rehearse in a simulated environment, such as practicing an emergency procedure. I would choose between them based on whether the real surroundings need to stay visible.

**Unit 5 Law** · 用于讨论未经许可下载电影应如何处罚。

> I think the response should reflect the scale and intent of the infringement. A warning and an explanation of legal viewing options could be appropriate for a first-time individual offender. Someone who repeatedly distributes large numbers of films for profit causes greater harm and may deserve a stronger penalty. This approach recognizes the work of creators and leaves room for proportionate consequences.

**Unit 6 Tomorrow** · 大学为什么参加没有奖金的超级高铁竞赛。下面是已用于该题的作答文本。

> Universities enter competitions like the Hyperloop challenge because students can test ideas under real constraints. A team has to build a prototype, document its tests, and learn from failures, which is hard to replicate in a lecture. The event also lets students compare designs with other teams and meet engineers who can offer feedback. Even without a prize, the prototype and the lessons from testing can support future projects.
